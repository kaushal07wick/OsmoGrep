## OsmoGrep 0.3.6

- A quieter update screen combines the approved Split and Quiet designs.
- Real download progress, connection retries, checksum verification, and a clear restart step.
- Chat stays hidden until you choose Later, Skip, or finish the update and restart.
- Compact provider logos, exact model IDs, and clickable model and provider selectors.
- The welcome logo and workspace header disappear when your conversation starts.

Update failures stay inside the updater with a retry option. Provider logos are bundled for offline display on terminals with Kitty graphics; other terminals retain readable provider names.

The updater already running handles this upgrade using its current interface. The new design takes effect after restarting into 0.3.6.
