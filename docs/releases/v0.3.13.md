## Workspace companions

- Open Mac and iPhone viewers beside chat through Settings → Workspace, using separately installed terminal-mac and terminal-phone companions.
- Give vision-capable models real screen observations through MCP, with phone actions bound to the selected target.
- Keep long conversations lighter with cached text formatting, and preserve unrelated settings when changing models or keys.

Companions require their own platform setup. Automatic device panes currently use Ghostty 1.3+ on Apple silicon macOS. Live device input has not been validated on the development machine because no Mac guest or iOS runtime is configured. Neovim/chat continuity and the approved original orb remain unchanged from 0.3.12.

The architecture map, audit and reproducible validation notes are in `docs/`.
