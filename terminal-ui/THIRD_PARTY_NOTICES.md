# Bundled terminal presentation dependencies

The generated renderer includes `@earendil-works/pi-tui` (MIT), `marked` (MIT)
and `get-east-asian-width` (MIT). Versions and source locations are pinned in
`package-lock.json`; legal comments are preserved in the generated bundle.
Their full license texts are reproduced in `bundle/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.
